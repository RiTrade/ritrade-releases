# RiTrade 1.2.35

_Released 2026-07-21_

### New features

- **See your trading day at whatever level of detail you want.** The "P&L by Hour" and "Hourly P&L" tiles are now one tile, **P&L by Time**, and it's no longer locked to whole hours: you choose the interval it groups your trades into -- 5, 15 or 30 minutes, an hour, or 4 hours -- so you can zoom right in on the stretch of the day that matters. Choose which times to show with one-tap market-session presets (Pre-market, Regular hours and After-hours, now set to the right hours) or add your own time windows down to the minute -- as many as you like, such as 1-3 AM and 5-6 AM. Flip the chart between vertical and horizontal from the tile's settings, and read every time in your dashboard's timezone -- the User Guide now spells out how that timezone shapes each time-of-day view. Any hour tiles already on your dashboards turn into the new one on their own, keeping the look they had.
