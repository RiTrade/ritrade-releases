# RiTrade 1.2.36

_Released 2026-07-22_

### New features

- **Get your data back without leaving the app.** RiTrade already saves a full copy of your data before every data upgrade and before every app update -- now you can bring one back right from Backup & restore, instead of hunting through folders and unzipping files. You see your own backups newest-first, each labelled by when and why it was saved ("Before a data upgrade", "Before updating to v1.2.35"); pick one, confirm, and RiTrade restores it and reopens on that data -- saving a copy of your current data first, so a restore you didn't mean to do is itself undoable. If a backup is unreadable or your data is in use, it stops safely and changes nothing.
