# RiTrade 1.2.24

_Released 2026-07-15_

### Bug fixes

- Your dashboard now recovers on its own if it stumbles while loading on startup. A rare glitch could previously leave the dashboard blank until you reloaded the app; now it quietly reloads and carries on, and in the rare case it genuinely can't, it shows a clear "reload to try again" message instead of leaving you staring at a blank screen.
