# RiTrade 1.2.38

_Released 2026-07-27_

### New features

- **RiTrade now spots when a stock you trade changes its ticker, and keeps it as one instrument.** When a company renames its ticker (FGMC becomes BXBL, say) your broker re-sends the same trades under the new name, and until now that quietly imported them a second time, doubling that stock's trade count and profit. RiTrade now sees that the two symbols share the exact same fills and asks you one plain question: which ticker does this stock use now? Pick it and the older one merges in, so the stock reads as one instrument everywhere and your counts and profit stay right. Choose "Import both" if they really are two different stocks, and RiTrade keeps them apart and never asks again. Nothing is deleted and nothing is decided without you: the new Symbol links screen in the menu undoes any choice and splits the two tickers cleanly back apart, and "Flip direction" there swaps which ticker is the current one if a link ever points the wrong way.
- **Duplicate a dashboard with a right-click.** Right-click any dashboard tab, choose Duplicate, and the familiar dashboard form opens already filled in from the original: the name with "(copy)" added, the same timezone and time period. Save lands the copy at the end of the tab strip with every tile intact, its settings, its size and position, and the custom title and look you gave it, along with the dashboard's filters. Trying a variant of a dashboard you have already built no longer means rebuilding it tile by tile, and the original is untouched.

### Improvements

- **Every tile now explains what its settings mean, the same way, in the same place.** Open Configure on any tile and the settings sit on the left with a plain-language explainer on the right, the way the Stat Card has always worked. On a tile that picks a metric the panel follows your choice. On the two Symbol Performance tiles it switches between the Net and the Gross explanation as you flip the basis, so the trap of a symbol that wins most of its trades and still loses you money is spelled out where you meet it. Everywhere else it carries that tile's own Description, Data and Edge, with room for the notes that used to be crammed in beside the controls.
- **Importing a plain broker CSV now starts on the right Quantity and Price columns.** When a file carries both the price you ordered at and the price you actually filled at, the import now picks the fill price, so your entry and exit prices (and the profit figures built on them) are right without you needing to know which column is which. It now also recognises a total or filled quantity column it used to leave blank, and prefers the quantity you actually filled over the size of the whole order, so a partly filled order is not counted as though it filled in full. You can still change any column yourself before importing.
- **The app's writing reads the way a person would say it.** Every explainer now opens with the question it answers and ends on the decision that follows, so one read is enough to know what to do next. The help on the two Symbol Performance tiles, which had grown into a wall of side detail, is the clearest case. Punctuation and capitals are consistent from one screen to the next. Nothing RiTrade tells you has changed meaning; it simply reads better.

### Bug fixes

- Deleting a folder account's trades and pressing Sync now brings them back. It used to be a dead end: once you cleared a watch-folder account's trades, nothing short of deleting and recreating the whole account got them in again. Now Sync re-imports on its own when the account still knows your columns and walks you into mapping when it does not, a full or partial delete refills exactly on the next Sync with no duplicates, and the delete screen tells you so.
- Adding another file to an account you have already set up now just works. The next file of the same kind used to be treated as brand new, sending you back to pick your column-name row and then dead-ending on a "file could not be read" wall. A returning file of a layout the account knows now imports in one confirming step, and a genuinely different file is recognised on its own terms or mapped like a first-time file, never forced into the old layout.
- Importing a broker file that mixes filled and cancelled orders no longer warns that a big chunk of it "couldn't be read". A Webull US Orders file lists every order you placed, including the ones that never filled, and a correct 25-trade import was announcing "32 rows couldn't be read". Cancelled orders are now counted separately in their own calm line and that warning is reserved for a row which genuinely should have imported, so what actually imports is unchanged: only the alarming count is gone.
- A mis-click on empty space in a chart no longer silently widens your whole dashboard. On the two Symbol Performance charts, clicking past the bars used to clear the drill-down on every tile at once, so a per-symbol subtotal quietly became a grand total that looked identical to it. That clear now respects the same "Filter other tiles" switch as everything else.
- Clicking the Expense Breakdown donut no longer wipes every filter on your dashboard. A slice click was meant to filter by that purpose and instead cleared the lot, so the click now leaves your other tiles alone, the help no longer promises otherwise, and the tile says "purpose" throughout.
- Every tile's Configure panel now tells the truth about whether that tile can filter the others. On 22 of the 30 tiles the Following tab's "Filter other tiles" switch had nothing listening: you could flick it, it saved, and it did nothing, because those tiles have no selection to pass on (a Stat Card is one number, so there is nothing in it to click). Those switches are now greyed with a line saying why and stay switched on, so your saved setting is untouched if that tile ever gains something to send, and three tiles that claimed the ability in the other direction are corrected too.
- The Slicer now obeys its own "Filter other tiles" switch, which used to leave the Slicer filtering your whole dashboard whether it was on or off. Its own controls now switch off with it rather than sitting there looking usable, and a line points back at the setting that brings them back.
- "Total Fees" and the figures beside it now name every cost they actually cover. The wording said commissions and ECN fees, while the number has always included everything your broker charged you: regulatory and clearing fees, tax, and every other charge passed on, which on a real account came to close to a sixth of the whole fee bill. Your figures have not moved and no total changed: the number was right all along, and it is the sentence describing it that has been corrected.
- The explanations no longer promise that your gross figure minus your net one lands on your fee total to the cent. It does not: fees are recorded to more decimal places than trade totals, so across a lot of trades the two can sit a few cents apart, and anyone trying to tie them out was hunting a gap the app had told them would not be there. The wording now says what is true, that fees are what turn your gross figure into your net one.
- Three explanations described something other than the number they sat under. "Current Streak" said it ran up to today, when it actually runs to the last day you traded inside your filter; "P/L Ratio (Median)" blamed the gap with its average purely on outliers, when the two also count breakeven trades differently; and "Win Rate" never showed the note it promised, that a trade closing exactly flat counts as a loss rather than a win. All three now read the way the figures behave.
- A tile's Configure panel now describes the metric you actually picked. Several perfectly valid choices used to show "This metric isn't available anymore", and a tile genuinely set to a retired metric fared worse: the panel quietly explained the first metric in the list instead, so you read about a figure you never picked and saving replaced your choice with that substitute. Now a valid metric gets its own explanation, the retired message is reserved for a metric that really is gone, and a tile holding one keeps your original choice in view until you pick a replacement yourself.
- The four switches on a tile's Following tab can now be identified by a screen reader, with the line explaining each one read out alongside it, instead of announcing as an unnamed checkbox.
- Creating a dashboard from a template ("Copy layout from") no longer quietly drops the custom titles and looks you had given the original's tiles: every tile now arrives with its title and appearance intact, the same faithful copy Duplicate makes.
- Setting a dashboard's time period to "This Month" now sticks, instead of reverting to "All Time" on every launch and showing far more history than you had asked for.
- These corrections reach you even if you have been using RiTrade for a while. Some of this help could only ever be refreshed on a brand-new installation, so an existing journal would have gone on showing the old wording for good, however many updates arrived. That is fixed, and the corrected explanations arrive on your next launch.
