# RiTrade 1.2.15

_Released 2026-06-27_

### Added

- **The P&L Ledger tile can expand your trading gains into one line per trade.**
  Turn on "Expand trades into individual lines" in the tile's settings and the
  Trading Gains section opens up to list every trade you closed in the period - its
  date, symbol, account, and what it made or lost, each shown with a + or - so
  winners and losers read clearly on any colour theme - above the same running
  total. The lines always add up to the Trading Gains subtotal, so breaking it out
  never changes the number; leave the switch off and it stays a single summary line.
  Previously the switch did nothing.

### Changed

- **Removed the P&L Calendar's "Trade dots before bar" setting.** Day cells show a
  win/loss ratio bar, and this setting no longer changed anything, so it has been
  taken out of the tile's settings.
